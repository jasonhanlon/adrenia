Why we’re building Adrenia

Adrenia is for women who enjoy taking care of themselves and want better information about what they buy.

Who it’s for

You might have a bathroom shelf full of things you love, or one serum you buy over and over. You might take supplements already or still be deciding whether any belong in your day. We’re building for women who want to make those choices for themselves.

What we want to get right

We’re starting with a small collection of supplements and skin care. Before we put a product on sale, we want its page to show the full label, explain what’s in it, and tell you how to use it. If we can’t answer a basic question about a formula, we have more work to do.
